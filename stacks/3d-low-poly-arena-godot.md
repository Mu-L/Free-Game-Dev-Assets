# Make a 3D low-poly arena game in Godot

For a solo developer building a small 3D arena game in Godot 4. The Tools picks are Godot 4 add-ons.

## Art

- **Arena pieces:** [Kenney Mini Arena](../catalog/3d/kenney-mini-arena.md). 20 miniature arena pieces in glTF, FBX and Blend.
- **Characters:** [Quaternius Universal Base Characters](../catalog/characters/quaternius-universal-base-characters.md). Six humanoid bases with humanoid rigs for retargeting in an engine, plus mix-and-match hairstyles.
- **Animations:** [Quaternius Universal Animation Library](../catalog/animation/quaternius-universal-animation-library.md). 120+ humanoid clips (locomotion, combat, emotes and more) on a retarget-friendly rig, with export sets tested for Godot.
- **Melee weapons:** [3dmodelscc0 Melee Weapons Pack](../catalog/3d/3dmodelscc0-melee-weapons.md). One pack of melee weapons in GLB. The entry covers this pack only; its sibling packs have their own entries.
- **Controller prompts:** [Kenney Input Prompts](../catalog/2d/kenney-input-prompts.md). 1500+ button glyphs for Xbox, PlayStation, Switch, Steam Deck, keyboard and mouse.

## Audio

- **Impact sounds:** [Kenney Impact Sounds](../catalog/audio/kenney-impact-sounds.md). 130 punches, thuds and physical impacts.
- **Menu sounds:** [Kenney UI Audio](../catalog/audio/kenney-ui-audio.md). 50 clicks and switches for menus, toggles, confirm and cancel.
- **Music:** [Abstraction Music Loop Bundle](../catalog/audio/tallbeard-abstraction-music-loop-bundle.md). 200+ music loops.

## Fonts

- **Menus and subtitles:** [Atkinson Hyperlegible](../catalog/fonts/atkinson-hyperlegible.md). A sans designed for low vision, with distinct B and 8, O and 0, and I, l and 1.

## Tools

- **Camera and shake:** [Phantom Camera](../catalog/tools/phantom-camera.md). A Godot 4 add-on for target tracking, transitions and camera shake.
- **Input remapping:** [G.U.I.D.E.](../catalog/tools/godot-guide.md). Input mapping and remapping for Godot 4, including controller configurations and Steam Deck rebinding.
- **Enemy logic:** [Beehave](../catalog/tools/beehave.md). Behavior trees for Godot 4 enemy and NPC logic, built as node hierarchies in the scene tree.
