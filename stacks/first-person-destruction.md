# Make a first-person destruction game

For a solo developer making a first-person game where things break apart. Debris is made ahead of time: fracture a mesh in Blender and ship the pieces, which works in any engine.

## Art

- **First-person arms:** [FPS Arms, rigged (OpenGameArt)](../catalog/characters/oga-fps-arms-rigged.md). A pair of first-person arms with a full hand rig, and the catalog's only rigged first-person arms. FBX and Blend only, so they go through Blender before most engines.
- **Industrial props:** [Kenney Factory Kit](../catalog/3d/kenney-factory-kit.md). 140 conveyor belts, machines and warehouse props for interiors; not building shells.
- **Concrete, metal and brick:** [ambientCG](../catalog/3d/ambientcg.md). 1500+ seamless PBR materials, with concrete, metal and brick among its tags.
- **Smoke and dust:** [Kenney Smoke Particles](../catalog/shaders-vfx/kenney-smoke-particles.md). 70 smoke and explosion frames for flipbooks and particle systems.

## Audio

- **Impact sounds:** [Kenney Impact Sounds](../catalog/audio/kenney-impact-sounds.md). 130 punches, thuds and physical impacts.
- **Ambience and foley:** [BigSoundBank](../catalog/audio/bigsoundbank.md). 3,500+ field and foley recordings. Check each file's marking on the site: the entry says which files are covered.

## Tools

- **Pre-broken debris:** [Cell Fracture (Blender extension)](../catalog/tools/blender-cell-fracture.md). Blender's fracture extension: it splits a mesh into convex pieces, the usual way pre-broken game debris is made.
- **3D editor:** [Blender](../catalog/tools/blender.md). The editor the fracture extension runs in, and the way into the arms' Blend and FBX files.
