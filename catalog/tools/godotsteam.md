# GodotSteam

GDExtension bindings to Steamworks for Godot 4 (achievements, stats, leaderboards, etc.). Docs/home: [godotsteam.com](http://godotsteam.com/). Free with Steam; subject to Valve’s Steamworks SDK terms and the addon’s own license. Both grants are unverified on this entry (`needs-review`). Do not treat Asset Library “free” as a shipping clearance.

## Notes

- Steamworks access requires a Steam partner account. The addon does not replace Valve’s SDK license.
- Godot 4 GDExtension: match the addon build to your engine minor version. A 4.2 build will not load in 4.3 without a rebuild.
