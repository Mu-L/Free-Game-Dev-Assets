# Make a 2D pixel platformer

For a solo developer making a side-view pixel platformer in any engine. The tiles, characters and background are drawn for a side view. Music comes as two picks: five looping tracks, and an 18-track album for more.

## Art

- **Tiles and level art:** [Kenney Pixel Platformer](../catalog/2d/kenney-pixel-platformer.md). 200 tiles and sprites on an 18x18 grid, covering terrain, characters and backgrounds.
- **Player character:** [LuizMelo Martial Hero](../catalog/2d/luizmelo-martial-hero.md). Animated sheets for idle, run, jump, fall, attacks, hit and death.
- **Enemies:** [LuizMelo Monsters Creatures Fantasy](../catalog/2d/luizmelo-monsters-creatures-fantasy.md). Four enemies (skeleton, mushroom, goblin, flying eye), each with idle, walk, attack, hit and death sheets.
- **Parallax background:** [Industrial Parallax Background (ansimuz)](../catalog/2d/ansimuz-industrial-parallax.md). A night-time industrial skyline in four seamless, looping layers, to move at different speeds.
- **Menus and HUD:** [Kenney UI Pack](../catalog/2d/kenney-ui-pack.md). 430 buttons, panels and sliders.

## Audio

- **Music, no credit:** [5 Chiptunes (Action), SubspaceAudio](../catalog/audio/subspaceaudio-5-chiptunes.md). Five looping tracks: Title Screen, Level 1, Level 2, Level 3 and Ending.
- **Music, with credit:** [Eric Skiff, Resistor Anthems](../catalog/audio/eric-skiff-resistor-anthems.md). An 18-track album of melodic 8-bit music, for more tracks than [5 Chiptunes](../catalog/audio/subspaceaudio-5-chiptunes.md) has.
- **Sound effects:** [ChipTone](../catalog/tools/chiptone.md). A sound-effect generator in the sfxr family that runs in the browser and exports WAV.

## Fonts

- **Title and score text:** [Press Start 2P](../catalog/fonts/press-start-2p.md). An arcade-style bitmap face for titles, scores and HUD text.

## Tools

- **Level editor:** [LDtk](../catalog/tools/ldtk.md). A 2D level editor with entity and layer workflows that exports JSON.
- **Pixel art editor:** [Pixelorama](../catalog/tools/pixelorama.md). A pixel art and animation editor with tileset features.
