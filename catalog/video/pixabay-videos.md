# Pixabay Videos

Pixabay's free stock video, under the same Pixabay Content License as its images and audio (see [pixabay-audio](../audio/pixabay-audio.md)). Free, no credit, modification allowed; the one rule that matters for a game is the ban on standalone redistribution.

## Notes

- **Use in a game:** the licence does not mention games. It is a general grant ("Use Content for free", "Modify or adapt Content into new works") with a short list of prohibited uses, none of which covers shipping a clip inside a game
- **Standalone ban:** you may not sell or distribute a clip "on a Standalone basis", meaning unchanged and on its own. Inside your game, where it is part of a larger work, is fine; a "video backgrounds pack" of Pixabay clips is not
- Clips showing recognisable trademarks, logos or brands may not be used commercially "in relation to goods and services"
- The licence puts third-party rights on you: people, property and brands in a clip may need their own consent
- The licence summary says only the full Content License is binding; the full text page rendered no licence body in a browser this pass, so the quotes below are from the summary

## Evidence

- Live [Content License Summary](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/) (2026-09-23): "Use Content for free", "Use Content without having to attribute the author", "Modify or adapt Content into new works"
- Same page (2026-09-23): "You cannot sell or distribute Content (either in digital or physical form) on a Standalone basis. Standalone means where no creative effort has been applied to the Content and it remains in substantially the same form as it exists on our website."
